Privacy Policy

mayor-of-agentown · Effective November 16, 2026

mayor-of-agentown (the “App”) is an app on Reddit’s Developer Platform operated by the mayor-of-agentown team (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what the App collects, why, who processes it, and how long we keep it. Questions or requests: message u/FirefighterSea4614 on Reddit.

What the App does

The App searches public Reddit posts for search phrases that we set, and lists matching threads in a private subreddit that we moderate. It also sends that list to our own database so our team can review it and decide where we can contribute a helpful answer. Any replies on Reddit are written and posted by individual people, by hand, from their own accounts. The App itself never posts, comments or votes anywhere except in our private subreddit.

Information the App collects

From public Reddit posts that match our search phrases, the App collects:

  • the post’s ID, link, title and text (we store a short excerpt of the text, not the full post);
  • the subreddit name, when it was posted, its score and its number of comments.

The App does not collect usernames, profile information, private messages, voting history, or any information that isn’t publicly visible on Reddit. Post text is written by Reddit users and may contain whatever they chose to share publicly; we only use it as described below.

The only people who use the App are members of our team who moderate our private subreddit. The App does not collect information about them beyond the settings they enter (such as search phrases).

How we use it

  • To show our team recent threads that match our search phrases.
  • To rank those threads by how likely it is that our team can give a genuinely helpful answer.

We do not use it for anything else.

Who processes it

  • Reddit hosts the App. The App keeps the IDs of threads it has already shown, so it doesn’t show them twice.
  • Supabase hosts our database, where the list of matching threads is stored.
  • OpenAI processes the title and text of matching posts through its API to score their relevance. Under OpenAI’s API policies, this data is not used to train its models, though OpenAI may keep it for a limited time to monitor for abuse.

What we never do

  • Sell, license or share Reddit data, or use it for advertising.
  • Use Reddit data to train, fine-tune or improve any AI or machine-learning model, or let anyone else do so.
  • Profile Reddit users or infer personal characteristics about them.
  • Contact Reddit users through the App.

How long we keep it

  • Thread information in our database is deleted within 30 days of being collected.
  • Thread IDs the App keeps to avoid duplicates are deleted after 30 days.
  • If a post is deleted or removed from Reddit, we delete what we stored about it as soon as we detect it, without waiting for the 30 days.

Your choices

If you posted something on Reddit and want us to delete anything we stored about it, message u/FirefighterSea4614 on Reddit with a link to the post and we will delete it.

Security

Access to stored data is limited to our team. Our database is protected by access controls and is not publicly readable.

Children

The App is not directed to anyone under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information about children.

Changes

If the App changes what it collects or how it uses it, we will update this page and its effective date.
